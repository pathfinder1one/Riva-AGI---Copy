## Active Projects in NextGen SuperComputing Club
The NextGen SuperComputing Club currently runs 4 primary active engineering projects:

1. **Riva-AGI**: Autonomous multimodal Artificial General Intelligence agent operating on NVIDIA DGX workstations with real-time bidirectional Gemini Live voice speech, LangGraph DAG multi-agent planning, and browser DOM automation.
2. **Autonomous Drone Vision & Tracking**: Deep learning perception models for unmanned aerial vehicle navigation and obstacle avoidance.
3. **Campus Medical Imaging & AI Diagnostics**: Accelerated healthcare imaging pipeline using PyTorch and CUDA.
4. **Smart Campus IoT & Telemetry**: Embedded telemetry monitoring energy and compute resources across the DGX Centre of Excellence.
